## Scope carve-outs (consistent with every prior Escrow/Closing screen)

- Manual entry throughout — no rate-table computation. The one standing exception (Tax Prorations' day-count math) doesn't recur here; nothing on these four pages is plain-arithmetic-only the way day-count is.
- **K. Payoffs and Payments** gets a plain list (Description/To/Amount) matching the SoftPro screenshot's outer grid — the per-line "Payoff Calculation" detail popup (principal balance, interest rate, per-diem, additional-interest-from/to dates) is genuine rate/loan-servicing calculation, out of scope here and left to the already-separate, not-yet-built "Payoff Calculations" nav placeholder (distinct item, not part of CDF).
- **No live cross-page aggregation.** Page 1's Costs at Closing / Cash to Close figures, Page 3's Calculating Cash to Close table, and Page 2's J total are all independently entered, same as Page 2 launched — `cdf_line`-style auto-pull across CDF pages stays future scope (already flagged after Page 2).
- **Page 1 simplified to the fixed-rate shape.** The real form supports multi-row adjustable-rate payment schedules ("Years 1-7", "Years 8-30", etc.) and conditional Prepayment Penalty/Balloon Payment sub-fields. This build captures one Loan Terms row and one Projected Payments row (fixed-rate shape, the overwhelming majority case) with plain Yes/No + explanation text fields for the "can this increase" questions, rather than modeling arbitrary ARM schedules — a deliberate simplification, flagged here rather than silently guessed at.
- **Page 4 is fixed disclosure text**, not user-authored — each subsection (Assumption, Demand Feature, etc.) is a small set of Yes/No radios plus one optional explanation field, matching the real form's own structure (it's a disclosure form, not a data-entry form).

## Schema

Four new tables, one per page (each one-row-per-order except Page 3's list sections), continuing migration numbering from `0028`:

**`0029_cdf_page1.sql` — `cdf_page1` (singleton per order, atomic upsert via `ensureCdfPage1Id`, matching `curative_settings`/`property_details`'s convention):**
loan_amount, interest_rate, monthly_principal_interest, principal_interest_can_increase (bool), principal_interest_increase_explanation, has_prepayment_penalty (bool), prepayment_penalty_max, has_balloon_payment (bool), balloon_payment_amount, estimated_total_monthly_payment, estimated_escrow_monthly, taxes_included_in_escrow (bool), homeowners_insurance_included_in_escrow (bool), other_escrow_included (bool), other_escrow_description, closing_costs_total, closing_costs_note, cash_to_close_total, cash_to_close_note.

**`0030_cdf_page3.sql`:**
- `cdf_cash_to_close` (singleton per order): loan_amount_estimate/final, closing_costs_j_estimate/final, closing_costs_paid_before_closing_estimate/final, payoffs_k_estimate/final, cash_to_close_estimate/final, cash_to_close_from_borrower (bool), cash_to_close_to_borrower (bool), closing_costs_financed — mirrors the screenshot's Loan Estimate/Final/"Did this change?" grid; the three "did this change" dropdowns become plain text/select fields (`loan_amount_changed`, `closing_costs_changed`, `payoffs_changed`, each `'Yes'|'No'`).
- `cdf_payoffs_payments` (list, same shape as `additional_title_charges`): order_id, sort_order, description, payee_contact_id, amount.
- `cdf_transaction_summary_lines` (list, covers both Borrower's and Seller's Summaries of Transactions): order_id, party ('Borrower'|'Seller'), section ('due_from_at_closing'|'due_to_at_closing'|'paid_already'|'due_from_before_closing'|'due_to_before_closing'|'adjustments'), sort_order, description, amount. One flat table with `party`+`section` discriminators, same "flatten templated structure" pattern as Tax Prorations and CDF Page 2 — the real form's Borrower's and Seller's tables share an identical shape, just mirrored.

**`0031_cdf_page4.sql` — `cdf_page4` (singleton):**
has_assumption (bool), assumption_allowed (bool), has_demand_feature (bool), demand_feature_explanation, has_negative_amortization (bool), negative_amortization_explanation, partial_payments_accepted (bool), partial_payments_explanation, has_security_interest (bool), security_interest_property_address, escrow_type ('Escrow'|'No Escrow'), escrow_initial_deposit, escrow_monthly_payment, no_escrow_estimated_property_costs, no_escrow_escrowed_note.

**`0032_cdf_page5.sql` — `cdf_page5` (singleton):**
total_of_payments, finance_charge, amount_financed, apr, total_interest_percentage, print_appraisal_disclosure (bool), liability_after_foreclosure ('state law may protect you'|'state law does not protect you'), plus one row per Contact Information column via a small child list `cdf_page5_contacts` (order_id, sort_order, role — 'Lender'|'Mortgage Broker'|'Real Estate Broker (B)'|'Real Estate Broker (S)'|'Settlement Agent'|'Additional', contact_id fk contacts, nmls_id, license_id, contact_person, contact_nmls_id, contact_license_id, email, phone) — matches the screenshot's 5-column-plus-"Additional Contacts" grid without hardcoding exactly 5 columns. Confirm Receipt (signature config) explicitly **not** built — it configures what prints on generated documents, and Genesis doesn't generate a printable CDF yet (same boundary as the existing ALTA-licensing note: no CDF this app produces reaches a real client while that status is unconfirmed).

RLS: standard blanket `authenticated` policy on every table, matching every prior migration.
